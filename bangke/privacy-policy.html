Bangke Pinyin Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026 · Last updated 16 November 2026

English · 中文

Bangke Pinyin (com.bangke.android) does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no servers, and does not request network access. Everything you type and all model inference stay on your device.

Information we collect

None. The developer operates no backend service and receives no data from the app. Bangke Pinyin contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and it does not request the Android INTERNET permission.

Information processed on your device

The following data is processed strictly on your device to provide input-method features. None of it is transmitted off your device.

DataPurposeWhere it goes
Keystrokes, pinyin, and handwriting strokes Produce Chinese and English text candidates Processed locally by the bundled Rime engine, T9 correction model, and handwriting recognition model. Never uploaded.
Text before the cursor (when Offline AI Candidates is enabled) Generate context-aware full-pinyin candidate suggestions Read locally on demand and fed only to the bundled offline model. Automatically disabled in password fields and editors requesting no personalized learning. Can be disabled in AI Settings.
Clipboard history (when Clipboard Listening is enabled) Display recent copied items for quick pasting Stored locally in the app's private database until deleted, cleared in the clipboard panel, or disabled in Settings.
Selected theme background image (optional) Customize keyboard background appearance Accessed only when you explicitly choose an image via the system picker and saved locally inside app storage.
Device contact names (optional) Prioritize matching contact names when typing full pinyin Read locally only if you grant the optional Contacts permission; kept in memory/local index and never sent off device.

Permissions

PermissionWhy Bangke Pinyin needs it

READ_CONTACTS (optional)Optional local lookup of contact names for pinyin candidate prioritization.

Data sharing

Bangke Pinyin does not share data with anyone. There are no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising partners, and no remote data processors, because no user data ever leaves your device. Note that system Emoji rendering relies on the Android system font service, which is governed by your device vendor's system policy; Bangke Pinyin never passes typed content to it.

Retention and deletion

Your preferences, Rime user dictionary, and clipboard history remain in local app storage on your device until you clear clipboard history in the app, reset app storage in Android system settings, or uninstall Bangke Pinyin. If you export a backup ZIP via "Other → Export user data", that file is saved only to the location you choose.

Children

Bangke Pinyin is a general-purpose keyboard utility. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone of any age.
